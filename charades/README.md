# Charades

Pick a topic (seven built in, plus your own from **Create a game**: name it, add your words), choose the time limit and the time boost for each correct answer, then hold the phone sideways to your forehead.

- **Right half** of the screen (or tilt the screen towards the floor): correct. **Left half** (or tilt towards the ceiling): pass. Tilting uses the phone's motion sensor; iPhones ask permission first, and taps always work.
- Correct and Pass show for a full second. At the end: how many correct and passed, and every word.
- Where the browser allows, it goes full screen, sideways, and keeps the screen on.

Your games are saved in this browser. Tests: `node charades/tests/run.mjs`.
